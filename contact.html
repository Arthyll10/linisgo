Before you reach out

A little clarity, before your clean.

Can I get a price before messaging?

Yes. Choose your home size and extras in our price estimator. Your final quote and availability are confirmed by the team.

What if I need to change an existing booking?

Contact the team with your booking name, scheduled date, and the change you need. We’ll need to confirm any new schedule with you.

When should I pay the downpayment?

Agree on your quote and schedule with the team first. A 50% downpayment confirms the slot; the balance is paid after the clean and your inspection.
