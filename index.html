How booking works

Five steps from message to spotless

Choose a step to see what happens next.

Step 1 of 5

Tell us about your home

Send your house size, location, preferred date, and whether you need a basic or deep clean. Mention any add-ons so we can prepare your quote.

Step 2 of 5

Know your price before you book

Review the proposed cleaning scope and price. Ask about anything you would like included before confirming your booking.

Step 3 of 5

Reserve a time that works for you

Agree on your cleaning schedule with our team, then make the 50% downpayment using the payment details provided for your booking.

Step 4 of 5

Leave the cleaning to us

Our team arrives at the agreed time to carry out your chosen clean. Point out any areas you would like us to pay particular attention to.

Step 5 of 5

Take a look around together

Inspect the cleaned areas with the team and raise any questions before settling the remaining balance on completion.
