A few useful details

Before you book

Which clean should I choose?

Choose Basic Clean for regular upkeep. Deep Clean includes everything in Basic, plus detailed attention to areas such as baseboards, door frames and hard-to-reach spots.

Can I combine add-ons with my clean?

Yes. Ironing, Refrigerator Interior Cleaning, and Laundry Folding & Organizing are available with a core cleaning booking. Each selection covers one block or unit; confirm additional scope with the team.

What should I prepare before the team arrives?

Secure valuables, keep pets safe, and provide access. Empty the refrigerator if you select interior cleaning. Our team brings the cleaning products and equipment needed for your service.

Where do you clean, and is same-day service available?

Based in Xevera, Mabalacat City — serving all of Mabalacat City. Scheduling and same-day requests depend on availability.

When do I need a tailored quote?

Bedroom count is an estimate input. Floor area, bathrooms, condition, and staffing affect your confirmed quote. Studios, 4+ bedrooms, unusually large or heavily soiled homes, multiple-cleaner jobs, and other unsupported combinations require a quote. Deep Clean for a 1-bedroom home requires a quote. All duration ranges are estimates, subject to scope.
