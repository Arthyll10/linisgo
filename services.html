A few useful details

Before you book

Which clean should I choose?

Choose Basic Clean for regular upkeep. Deep Clean includes everything in Basic, plus detailed attention to areas such as baseboards, door frames and hard-to-reach spots.

Can I combine add-ons with my clean?

Yes. Request ironing, the All-in-One supplies add-on, or both with your Basic or Deep Clean. Confirm the scope and any additional cost with the team before booking.

What should I prepare before the team arrives?

Have the equipment above ready for a standard booking. If you request the All-in-One Add-on, confirm the supplies the team will bring. Share any access instructions and areas needing special attention in your booking message.
